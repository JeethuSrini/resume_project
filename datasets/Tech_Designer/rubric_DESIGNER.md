# Resume Evaluation Rubric — Graphic Designer

Use this rubric to score each dimension from 1 to 5. Be strict: only credit explicitly stated information. Note: because resumes do not include portfolio links, do not penalize for absence of portfolio beyond what is noted in the Certifications/Extracurricular dimension.

---

## Work Experience (1–5)
Recruiters for graphic design roles at brand/marketing agencies prioritize professional experience across both print and digital formats, client-facing work, and the ability to manage multiple deadlines.

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | 2+ years professional design experience in an agency or comparable client-facing setting, with explicit work in BOTH print and digital formats, and evidence of multi-project or deadline management |
| 4 | 2+ years design experience, but limited to mostly print or mostly digital (not both), OR less than 2 years in an agency/client-facing context |
| 3 | 1–2 years design experience with limited client-facing context or only internal/in-house work |
| 2 | Some design experience but primarily personal projects or freelance, or experience in adjacent fields (marketing, communications) without explicit design output |
| 1 | No relevant professional design experience |

---

## Technical Skills (1–5)
Core tools for this role: Adobe Illustrator, Adobe InDesign, Adobe Photoshop (all three are expected), Figma, and experience preparing production-ready files for print vendors. Web/digital asset creation is also valued.

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Explicitly lists all three core Adobe apps (Illustrator, InDesign, Photoshop) plus Figma, and mentions print production or file prep for vendors |
| 4 | Proficient in at least two of the three core Adobe apps with some print or digital production experience |
| 3 | Adobe tools mentioned but without naming specific apps, or only one core Adobe application listed |
| 2 | Generic "design software" or "graphic design tools" mentioned without specifics, or only outdated tools |
| 1 | No design software skills mentioned |

---

## Educational Background (1–5)
A bachelor's degree in Graphic Design, Visual Communications, or a closely related creative field is preferred by employers in this category.

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Bachelor's or higher in Graphic Design, Visual Communications, or a directly related design field |
| 4 | Bachelor's in a related creative field (e.g., Fine Arts, Advertising, Marketing, UX/Interaction Design) |
| 3 | Associate's degree in design or partial completion of a relevant design program |
| 2 | Some college coursework in design, or self-taught with demonstrable professional credentials |
| 1 | No design education mentioned, or exclusively unrelated field |

---

## Certifications / Extracurricular (1–5)
Valued extras for this role include: a portfolio or portfolio link, motion graphics experience (After Effects, Premiere), HTML/CSS for web handoff, and environmental or dimensional signage design experience.

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Portfolio mentioned/linked AND demonstrates 2+ additional skill areas: motion graphics, HTML/CSS, or environmental/signage design |
| 4 | Portfolio mentioned/linked AND 1 additional relevant skill area from the above |
| 3 | Portfolio mentioned without additional skills, OR additional relevant skills mentioned without a portfolio reference |
| 2 | Some extracurricular creative work mentioned but no portfolio and no additional relevant skill areas |
| 1 | No portfolio, certifications, or extracurricular activities relevant to graphic design mentioned |
