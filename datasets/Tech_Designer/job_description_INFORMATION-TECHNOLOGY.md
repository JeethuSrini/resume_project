# Information Technology Manager
**Company:** Vantara Solutions  
**Location:** Austin, TX (Hybrid)  
**Type:** Full-time  
**Salary:** $90,000 – $120,000/year  

---

## About Us
Vantara Solutions is a regional managed services and IT consulting firm supporting mid-market clients across healthcare, finance, and government sectors. Our team manages complex infrastructure environments and delivers technology strategy, systems administration, and security services for a growing client base.

---

## What You'll Do
- Oversee day-to-day IT operations including network infrastructure, servers, workstations, and cloud environments
- Manage and mentor a team of IT support staff and systems administrators
- Lead IT project planning and implementation, including system upgrades, migrations, and new deployments
- Develop and enforce IT policies, procedures, and security standards across the organization
- Analyze and mitigate technology risk; maintain compliance with relevant regulatory frameworks (HIPAA, NIST, etc.)
- Manage vendor relationships, software licensing, and IT budget planning
- Serve as escalation point for complex technical issues and outages
- Produce regular reporting on system performance, incidents, and project status for senior leadership
- Coordinate with business stakeholders to align technology solutions with organizational goals

---

## What We're Looking For
- 5+ years of experience in IT operations, systems administration, or a related technical role
- 2+ years in an IT management or team lead capacity
- Strong working knowledge of network infrastructure (LAN/WAN, VPN, firewalls, routers)
- Experience administering Windows Server, Active Directory, and Microsoft 365 environments
- Familiarity with cybersecurity principles, vulnerability management, and incident response
- Demonstrated ability to manage projects end-to-end using SDLC or similar methodologies
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with attention to cost-effective solutions
- Excellent communication skills for cross-functional collaboration and executive reporting
- Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or related field preferred

---

## Nice to Have
- Relevant certifications (CompTIA Security+, MCSE, CISSP, PMP, or equivalent)
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP)
- Background supporting government or defense IT environments
- Experience with data center operations or disaster recovery planning
