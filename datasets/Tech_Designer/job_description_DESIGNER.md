# Graphic Designer
**Company:** Meridian Creative Group  
**Location:** New York, NY (Hybrid)  
**Type:** Full-time  
**Salary:** $65,000 – $85,000/year  

---

## About Us
Meridian Creative Group is a mid-sized brand and marketing agency working with clients across retail, real estate, and consumer goods. We produce everything from brand identities and print collateral to digital campaigns and environmental signage.

---

## What You'll Do
- Design print and digital materials including brochures, signage, advertisements, social media assets, and email templates
- Develop logos, brand identities, and visual systems in line with client brand guidelines
- Produce production-ready files for print vendors and fabricators
- Create mockups, sketches, and presentation decks for client review cycles
- Collaborate with copywriters, account managers, and external vendors to bring projects from concept to final deliverable
- Maintain organized asset libraries and version control across multiple client projects
- Support web design requests including landing pages and web banners

---

## What We're Looking For
- 2+ years of professional graphic design experience
- Proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite (Illustrator, InDesign, Photoshop); Figma a plus
- Strong portfolio demonstrating work across both print and digital formats
- Experience preparing and submitting files for large-format or commercial print production
- Ability to manage multiple deadlines in a fast-paced environment
- Strong communication skills for client-facing presentations
- Bachelor's degree in Graphic Design, Visual Communications, or related field preferred

---

## Nice to Have
- Experience with motion graphics or video editing (After Effects, Premiere)
- Familiarity with HTML/CSS for web asset handoff
- Background in environmental or dimensional signage design
