# Resume Evaluation Rubric — IT Manager

Use this rubric to score each dimension from 1 to 5. Be strict: only credit explicitly stated information.

---

## Work Experience (1–5)
Recruiters for IT Manager roles prioritize depth of operational IT experience and demonstrated management responsibility.

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | 5+ years in IT operations/systems administration AND 2+ years in an IT management or team lead role; bonus for experience in the hiring sector (healthcare, government, or finance) |
| 4 | 5+ years IT operations with some management exposure, OR strong management experience with slightly under 5 years of operations |
| 3 | 3–5 years IT operations with minimal or no management experience |
| 2 | Fewer than 3 years of IT operations experience, or experience in IT-adjacent roles not directly relevant to infrastructure/systems management |
| 1 | No relevant IT experience, or only peripheral or non-technical work history |

---

## Technical Skills (1–5)
Key technical areas for this role: network infrastructure (LAN/WAN, VPN, firewalls, routers), Windows Server / Active Directory / Microsoft 365, cybersecurity and vulnerability management, cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP), and project management methodology (SDLC or equivalent).

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Explicitly covers all four areas: networking, Windows/AD/M365, cybersecurity, and cloud or project methodology |
| 4 | Strong coverage of most technical areas; missing only one key area |
| 3 | Core infrastructure skills present but limited breadth (e.g., only Windows admin, or only networking) |
| 2 | Some IT skills mentioned but without depth or breadth across required areas |
| 1 | Only generic or soft skills; no relevant IT technical skills listed |

---

## Educational Background (1–5)
A bachelor's degree in IT, Computer Science, or a closely related field is preferred by employers in this category.

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Bachelor's or higher in Information Technology, Computer Science, or a directly related technical field |
| 4 | Bachelor's in a related field (e.g., Engineering, Mathematics) or Associate's degree in IT/CS with substantial compensating experience |
| 3 | Some college or Associate's degree in an unrelated field, or self-taught background with demonstrated credentials |
| 2 | High school diploma or GED, with certifications partially compensating |
| 1 | No educational credentials mentioned, or exclusively non-technical education |

---

## Certifications / Extracurricular (1–5)
Top certifications for IT Manager roles include: CompTIA Security+, CISSP, MCSE, PMP, CAPM, CRISC, and CITM. Evidence of professional development or leadership activities is also valued.

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Holds 2 or more relevant certifications from the list above, with evidence of professional leadership or continuing education |
| 4 | Holds 1 relevant management- or security-level certification from the list above |
| 3 | Holds an entry-level IT certification (e.g., CompTIA A+, Network+) but not a management- or security-level cert |
| 2 | Certifications mentioned but not relevant to this role, or only expired/basic vendor certs |
| 1 | No certifications or professional development activities mentioned |
